Implementing a learning management system before establishing a performance management foundation often results in disconnected learning that produces completion metrics rather than performance impact. Organizations should first establish clear goals, enable ongoing performance conversations, and map skills gaps before launching an LMS so development activities are meaningfully tied to business outcomes.
