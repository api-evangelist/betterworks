Talent density, the concentration of quality skills and capabilities aligned to business outcomes, has become a measurable driver of financial performance. Traditional performance management systems fail to build it because they are episodic and static, whereas organizations need continuous intelligence about skills, goals, and manager coaching to execute strategy faster and deploy talent effectively.
